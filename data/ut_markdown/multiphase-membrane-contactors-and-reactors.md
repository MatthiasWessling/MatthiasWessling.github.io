# Multiphase membrane contactors and reactors

Author: J.M. Jani
Record: https://research.utwente.nl/en/publications/multiphase-membrane-contactors-and-reactors/
DOI: 10.3990/1.9789036532952

## Abstract

This thesis describes research work about multiphase contacting/reactions using porous membranes. Membrane based gas-liquid contacting offers many benefits, such as stable operation, easy fabrication and sustainable module design. Furthermore, accurate reaction control, higher mass transport due to enhanced surface-to-volume ratios and continuous operation are other key advantages of membrane based multiphase contacting/reaction.
