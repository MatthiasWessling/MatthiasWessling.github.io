## Focus

Amovion develops flow-electrode capacitive deionization (FCDI) to separate and reconcentrate salts from water. By moving ions rather than water, the process targets high recovery, fouling resistance at elevated salinity, and selective salt recycling—from purification and salt metathesis to resource recovery.

## Context

The venture builds on more than a decade of electrochemical membrane research in the Wessling group, including early flow-CDI work and the BMBF project ElektroWirbel. The founding team (including Christian Linnartz, Niklas Köller, Max Zimmermann, and Kerstin Brökelmann) received an EXIST Business Start-up Grant in 2024. Matthias Wessling serves on the scientific board.
