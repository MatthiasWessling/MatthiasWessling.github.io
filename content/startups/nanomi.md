## Focus

NANOMI grew out of membrane and microsieve research at the University of Twente. The company develops nano- and micrometer-scale membranes and devices for controlled liquid/liquid droplet formation—an enabling route to uniform microparticles and related formulation processes.

## Context

This was one of several early-2000s Twente spin-outs connected to the Membrane Science and Technology community. The technical theme—precision pore structures for multiphase processing—remains central to later work on structured membranes and process intensification.
