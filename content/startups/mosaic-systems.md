## Focus

MOSAIC SYSTEMS developed mixed matrix membranes for membrane chromatography—combining adsorptive functionality with membrane formats suited to bioseparation and related purification tasks.

## Context

Matthias Wessling served as founder and CTO. The venture is part of the early Twente spin-out wave that translated lab membrane concepts into product-oriented platforms for separation and chromatography.
