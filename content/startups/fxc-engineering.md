## Focus

FXC Engineering develops modular electrochemical hardware—notably the flex-X-cell / flex-E-cell platform—for research and industrial electrification. The cells are designed to be adaptable and scalable across electrochemical synthesis, water electrolysis, membrane processes, and related applications.

## Context

The founding team met at the Chair of Chemical Process Engineering (AVT.CVT) at RWTH Aachen University. Ongoing collaboration includes projects such as addEchem (additively manufactured electrochemical cell components), with simulation and testing support from AVT.CVT and Matthias Wessling.
