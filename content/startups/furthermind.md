## Focus

FURTHERMIND addresses research data management—organizing, documenting, and sharing experimental and project data so that collaborative science remains reproducible and reusable.

## Context

Listed among later Aachen-era startup activities. The need is practical: membrane and electrochemical campaigns generate large, multi-instrument datasets that benefit from structured capture and long-term access.
