## Focus

CON-VERGENCE develops fluid handling systems for membrane filtration—equipment and control platforms that help laboratories and process developers run membrane experiments with better reproducibility and process insight.

## Context

Originating from the University of Twente Membrane Science and Technology environment, Convergence is often cited among Twente membrane spin-offs. The company later became part of the Demcon group (Demcon Convergence), continuing to serve research and industrial membrane workflows.
