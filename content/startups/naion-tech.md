## Focus

naion.tech is an AVT spin-off developing customized nanofiltration membranes for recovering critical raw materials such as lithium, cobalt, and phosphorus. Thin (~150 nm) coatings are applied to commercial modules, guided by process-specific design—including machine-learning-assisted coating selection—to raise purity while reducing energy demand.

## Context

The team (including Ilka Rose, Matthias Heßelmann, Leon Schneider, and Milan Abel) is scaling from laboratory to pilot via the RWTH Innovation Sprint. Longer-term aims include targeting persistent contaminants such as PFAS, heavy metals, and pharmaceutical residues. The technology rests on more than ten years of membrane research at Aachen Chemical Engineering.
