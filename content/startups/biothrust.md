## Focus

BioThrust develops bionic bioreactors for bubble-free fermentation and cell production, aiming at scalable and controllable manufacturing for cell therapies and related bioprocesses.

## Context

Matthias Wessling serves as scientific advisor. The technology theme connects membrane-mediated gas–liquid contact to bioprocess scale-up—an area long pursued at AVT / DWI through hollow-fiber and membrane aeration concepts.
