## Focus

LIGHTMOTIF designs and develops surface-structured materials using laser ablation and follow-on surface modification. The company provides precision micromachining capabilities for functional surfaces and related production systems.

## Context

Linked to the University of Twente innovation ecosystem of the early 2000s, Lightmotif sits at the interface of materials processing and structured interfaces—themes that also appear in membrane and microfluidic device fabrication.
