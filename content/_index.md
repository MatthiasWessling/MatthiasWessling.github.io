On these pages, I share notes and updates on:
- the research our teams conduct at RWTH Aachen University and the DWI Leibniz Institute for Interactive Materials
- [graduates](/graduates/), their theses and papers—often the product of team science across students, postdocs and collaborators
- projects I am interested in and currently working on
- startup activities from the past, present, and future

A recurring interest is mixed ionic–electronic materials and their assemblies—soft, permeable objects whose ionic and electronic states can store and transform recent history. I think of such systems as information materials: function arises from how charge, transport and contacts co-evolve under operation, not only from a static composition. Related overviews are collected under [Research](/research/), including notes on [PEDOT:PSS](/research/pedot/) and a [modelling roadmap](/research/miec-prc-modelling-roadmap/).
