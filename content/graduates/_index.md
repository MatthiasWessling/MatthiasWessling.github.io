Browse PhD graduates supervised as first thesis advisor. Use the filters to search by **name**, **topics**, **graduation year**, or **institution** (RWTH / Twente). Entries are grouped by year.

The directory combines [RWTH Publications](https://publications.rwth-aachen.de/search?ln=de&cc=PhDThesis&sc=1&p=Wessling+Dissertation) theses and earlier University of Twente dissertations listed in [UT Research Information](https://research.utwente.nl/) (second-examiner theses are omitted).
