## Abstract

Scaffold In article number 2302957, Laura De Laporte, Andrij Pich, and co-workers establish a cell-induced interlinking method for MAP scaffold formation, eliminating the need for chemical crosslinkers or pre-engineered pores to generate micro- or macropores within these 3D structures. This technique allows cells to autonomously arrange themselves alongside microgels, forming dynamic tissue constructs.
