## Abstract

Carbon Nanofibers In article 2200408, Matthias Wessling and co-workers demonstrate that carbon nanofibers are grown on a nickel foam as a binder-free electrode for electrochemical advanced oxidation processes. The C/Ni combination provides excellent reduction rates of O2 to H2O2, which is realized by multiple reaction pathways, being the two-electron pathway for carbon materials and a one-electron pathway through •O2 radical formation on the Ni nanoparticles embedded in the fibers.
