## Abstract

• CNT matrix incorporates PAC particles—offering a freestanding microtubular adsorbent. • The adsorbent presents a large surface area and adequate separation properties. • PAC boosts adsorption of antibiotic sulfamethoxazole at low concentrations. • The saturated adsorbent is regenerated through the Fenton chemistry and reused. • Elevated temperature shows a positive impact on regeneration efficiency.
