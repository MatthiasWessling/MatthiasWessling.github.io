## Abstract

The cover picture shows a compilation of microstructures that were prepared by phase separation micromolding. The top three images depict examples of two porous microstructures and one that is completely dense. The bottom picture shows optical reflection, caused by a periodic microstructure on a mold, and two transparent replicas that are floating in nonsolvent after phase separation. For more information, please read the Full Paper "Phase Separation Micromolding: A New Generic Approach for Microstructuring Various Materials" by R. G. H. Lammertink and co‐workers on page 645 ff.
