Research overviews documenting thematic lines in membranes, soft matter, and electrochemical systems. Across these notes, material function is treated as an operated state: fields and architecture reshape one another, and history is stored in composition, transport pathways and contacts. The entries are written for scientific evaluation—methods, theses, and key papers—rather than promotional narrative.
