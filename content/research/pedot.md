PEDOT:PSS is a soft mixed ionic–electronic conductor available as an aqueous dispersion. PEDOT provides electronic conduction along conjugated chains; PSS and the hydrated network support ionic transport and fixed-charge partitioning of cations. In our group it has been processed into porous particles, microfibers and hollow fibers—architected permeable units—and applied in electrochemical modules, biointerfaces and organic neuromorphic devices. Microfluidic and wet-spinning methods used for membrane and soft-matter fabrication transfer naturally to shaping these electro-ionic bodies. This note summarizes that body of work.

## Particles, microfibers, and hollow fibers

*[Porous PEDOT:PSS Particles and their Application as Tunable Cell Culture Substrate](https://doi.org/10.1002/admt.202100836)* (Bell, Jain, Rahimi, Felder, Linkhorst, Wessling, 2022) introduced a microfluidic emulsion process: PEDOT:PSS droplets in 1-octanol, followed by coagulation and partial crystallization in an isopropanol / sulfuric acid bath. Crystallinity, diameter, pore size, and mechanical and electrochemical properties depend on the IPA:SA ratio. The particles serve as tunable cell-culture substrates—soft units whose permeability, mechanics and charge storage are set together.

*[Wet-Spun PEDOT/CNT Composite Hollow Fibers as Flexible Electrodes for H2O2 Production](https://doi.org/10.1002/celc.202100237)* (Cui, Bell, Wang, Mohseni, Felder, Lölsberg, Wessling, 2021) continuously fabricated PEDOT:PSS and PEDOT:PSS/CNT hollow fibers. Acid-treated PEDOT:PSS was shown to be active for oxygen reduction to H₂O₂; with 40 wt% CNT, production rates of about 0.01 mg min⁻¹ cm⁻² and current efficiencies up to 54% were reported, with about 95% current retention after 20 h.

*[Microfluidic Spinning of PEDOT:PSS Microfibers for Nerve Guidance Conduits](https://doi.org/10.1002/admt.202401996)* (Paul, Rauer, Koulchitsky, Lampert, Linkhorst, Wessling, 2025) used an in-chip laser-written nozzle to set fiber diameters from about 1.2 to 3 µm. Mechanical properties were in the range of fibrin fibers relevant to nerve regeneration.

Siqi Wang’s thesis, *[Porous conductive polymer PEDOT:PSS for electrochemical applications](https://publications.rwth-aachen.de/record/1021247)* (2025), addresses pore architecture, ionic/electronic conductivity, and device performance of porous PEDOT structures. Particle fabrication is also covered by [WO2022034082A1](/patents-products/wo2022034082a1-conductive-pedot-pss-particles/). Geometry, porosity and state location (volumetric versus interfacial conduction) become controlled variables for subsequent assembly.

## Flow electrodes and electrosynthesis

*[PEDOT:PSS-CNT Composite Particles Overcome Contact Resistances in Slurry Electrodes for Flow-Electrode Capacitive Deionization](https://doi.org/10.1002/adfm.202303606)* (Rauer, Wang, Köller, Bell et al., 2023) examined PEDOT:PSS–CNT particles as alternatives to activated-carbon slurries in FCDI. Carbon slurries are limited by sedimentation, aggregation, and solids loading; charge transfer depends on particle contacts. Conductive hydrogel composite particles were investigated to reduce contact resistance. Electrical function then emerges from evolving contacts, conductive pathways and particle configurations rather than from a predefined circuit—a dynamic network whose conductivity depends on arrangement, interaction and history.

The wet-spun hollow fibers for H₂O₂ provide flexible tubular electrodes that combine electronic pathways (PEDOT, CNT) with aqueous oxygen reduction in a hollow-fiber geometry.

## Cell culture and nerve guidance

The porous particles of [2022](https://doi.org/10.1002/admt.202100836) were developed as cell-culture substrates with adjustable mechanics and electrochemistry. The microfluidic microfibers of [2025](https://doi.org/10.1002/admt.202401996) target nerve guidance conduits as continuous conductive filaments at micrometer length scales. In both cases PEDOT:PSS is used as a processable, ionically open soft material within biofabrication settings—permeable electro-ionic matter interfacing living systems through gradients and hydration.

## Organic neuromorphic devices

PEDOT:PSS thin-film devices can store programmable conductance states in aqueous electrolytes. *[Coupled ionic–electronic charge transport in organic neuromorphic devices](https://doi.org/10.1002/adts.202100492)* (Felder, Femmer, Bell, Rall, Linkhorst, Wessling, 2022) couples two-phase charge transport to self-discharge from impurities, accounting for nonideal writes and drift. Ionic migration, electronic conduction and electrochemical self-discharge jointly set accessible and fading material states—memory as a transport-resolved property of the permeable film. *[Reminding forgetful organic neuromorphic device networks](https://doi.org/10.1088/2634-4386/ac9c8a)* (2022) and *[Spiking neural networks compensate for weight drift in organic neuromorphic device networks](https://doi.org/10.1088/2634-4386/accd90)* (2023) examine network design, reminder pulses, and always-on spiking learning as responses to electrochemical drift. Material drift need not simply be eliminated; network strategies can be designed around the intrinsic dynamics of imperfect physical memory.

Daniel Felder’s thesis, *[Electrochemical charge transport in organic neuromorphic device networks](https://publications.rwth-aachen.de/record/964209)*, documents this program in detail. A related overview of neural networks in the group is given in *[Neural networks in membrane science](/research/from-spectra-to-synapses/)*.

## Forms and applications

| Form | Application | Representative work |
|---|---|---|
| Porous particles | Cell-culture substrates; FCDI slurry electrodes | [2022](https://doi.org/10.1002/admt.202100836); [2023](https://doi.org/10.1002/adfm.202303606) |
| Microfibers | Soft conductive scaffolds | Nerve guidance conduits ([2025](https://doi.org/10.1002/admt.202401996)) |
| Hollow fibers | Flexible tubular electrodes | H₂O₂ ORR ([2021](https://doi.org/10.1002/celc.202100237)) |
| Device films | Mixed ionic–electronic synapses | Neuromorphic transport and co-design |

The connecting theme is aqueous processing of a mixed ionic–electronic polymer into defined geometries, then use of coupled ionic, electronic and contact states in electrochemical, biological or neuromorphic contexts. Soft constituents supply the responsive electro-ionic medium; architecture—particle porosity, fiber geometry, packing contacts—determines how that state propagates and is read.
