PEDOT:PSS is easy to underestimate. On a shelf it is a commercial aqueous dispersion. In our laboratory it became something rarer: a **single mixed ionic–electronic polymer** that can be shaped into particles, microfibers, and hollow fibers — then asked to culture cells, desalinate water, make hydrogen peroxide, and store synaptic weights.

That breadth is not accidental. PEDOT:PSS conducts electrons along conjugated chains while PSS and the aqueous gel network host ions. Soft-matter processing (microfluidics, wet spinning, emulsion coagulation) sits naturally next to membrane and electrochemical engineering. The material meets our group where soft matter, transport, and devices already talk to each other.

## Shape first: particles, fibers, tubes

The enabling step was learning to **form** PEDOT:PSS at will, not only cast films.

*[Porous PEDOT:PSS Particles and their Application as Tunable Cell Culture Substrate](https://doi.org/10.1002/admt.202100836)* (Bell, Jain, Rahimi, Felder, Linkhorst, Wessling, 2022) introduced a microfluidic emulsion route: PEDOT:PSS droplets in 1-octanol, then coagulation and partial crystallization in an isopropanol / sulfuric acid bath. Crystallinity, diameter, pore size, and mechanical and electrochemical properties track the IPA:SA ratio. Pure PEDOT:PSS geometries were no longer limited to sheets and fibers — and the particles double as tunable cell-culture substrates.

The same shaping instinct runs through wet spinning. *[Wet-Spun PEDOT/CNT Composite Hollow Fibers as Flexible Electrodes for H2O2 Production](https://doi.org/10.1002/celc.202100237)* (Cui, Bell, Wang, Mohseni, Felder, Lölsberg, Wessling, 2021) continuously fabricates flexible PEDOT:PSS and PEDOT:PSS/CNT hollow fibers in one bottom-up step. Acid-treated PEDOT:PSS turns out to be catalytically active for oxygen reduction to H₂O₂; with 40 wt% CNT the fibers reach substantial production rates and retain ~95% current after 20 h.

Microfluidic spinning then reaches the micrometer scale relevant to soft tissue. *[Microfluidic Spinning of PEDOT:PSS Microfibers for Nerve Guidance Conduits](https://doi.org/10.1002/admt.202401996)* (Paul, Rauer, Koulchitsky, Lampert, Linkhorst, Wessling, 2025) uses an in-chip laser-written nozzle to set fiber diameters from ~1.2 to 3 µm. Mechanical properties fall in the range of fibrin fibers that matter for nerve regeneration — conductive soft matter as a structural cue, not only as a wire.

Siqi Wang’s thesis, *[Porous conductive polymer PEDOT:PSS for electrochemical applications](https://publications.rwth-aachen.de/record/1021247)* (2025), collects the electrochemical materials thread: pore architecture, ionic/electronic conductivity, and device performance across porous PEDOT structures.

IP follows the particle platform: [WO2022034082A1](/patents-products/wo2022034082a1-conductive-pedot-pss-particles/) covers methods for producing conductive PEDOT:PSS particles from aqueous mixtures.

## Soft electrodes: from slurry contacts to peroxide

Once shaped, PEDOT:PSS can replace or reinforce carbon in electrochemical modules where **particle–particle contact** or **tubular gas access** limits performance.

*[PEDOT:PSS-CNT Composite Particles Overcome Contact Resistances in Slurry Electrodes for Flow-Electrode Capacitive Deionization](https://doi.org/10.1002/adfm.202303606)* (Rauer, Wang, Köller, Bell et al., 2023) attacks a stubborn FCDI bottleneck. Activated-carbon slurries sediment, aggregate, and resist high solids loadings; charge transfer lives or dies by particle contacts. PEDOT:PSS–CNT composite particles offer a conductive hydrogel alternative that mitigates contact resistance and reframes what a flow electrode can be made of.

The wet-spun hollow fibers for H₂O₂ close a different loop: flexible tubular electrodes that combine electronic pathways (PEDOT, CNT) with aqueous electrochemistry in a geometry membrane laboratories already know how to spin and module.

## Living interfaces: cells and nerves

Biocompatibility and tissue-like elasticity are not marketing lines for PEDOT:PSS — they are design targets.

The porous particles of [2022](https://doi.org/10.1002/admt.202100836) are explicitly cell-culture substrates with tunable mechanics and electrochemistry. The microfluidic microfibers of [2025](https://doi.org/10.1002/admt.202401996) aim at nerve guidance conduits: continuous conductive filaments whose diameter and stiffness can be dialed toward biological length scales. Here PEDOT:PSS is not an electrode bolted onto biology. It is a soft, processable, ionically open material that can sit *inside* biofabrication workflows.

## Memory in wet polymers: neuromorphic PEDOT

The most conceptual leap treats PEDOT:PSS synapses as what they electrochemically are: **programmable conductance states in an aqueous mixed conductor**.

*[Coupled Ionic–Electronic Charge Transport in Organic Neuromorphic Devices](https://doi.org/10.1002/adts.202100492)* (Felder, Femmer, Bell, Rall, Linkhorst, Wessling, 2022) couples two-phase charge transport to self-discharge from impurities — explaining nonideal writes and drift that pure electronic models miss. *[Reminding forgetful organic neuromorphic device networks](https://doi.org/10.1088/2634-4386/ac9c8a)* (2022) and *[Spiking neural networks compensate for weight drift in organic neuromorphic device networks](https://doi.org/10.1088/2634-4386/accd90)* (2023) then ask how algorithms should live with that electrochemistry: reminder pulses, network design, and always-on spiking learning that can turn forgetfulness into a feature.

Daniel Felder’s thesis, *[Electrochemical charge transport in organic neuromorphic device networks](https://publications.rwth-aachen.de/record/964209)*, is the deep cut: PEDOT:PSS as hardware whose imperfections are modeled, not wished away. (That story also appears in our research note *[From spectra to synapses](/research/from-spectra-to-synapses/)*.)

## One polymer, four jobs

| Form | What it enables | Representative work |
|---|---|---|
| Porous particles | Tunable soft substrates; slurry electrodes | Cell culture particles; FCDI PEDOT–CNT composites |
| Microfibers | Soft conductive scaffolds at µm scale | Nerve guidance conduits |
| Hollow fibers | Flexible tubular electrodes | H₂O₂ ORR electrosynthesis |
| Thin-film / device films | Mixed ionic–electronic synapses | Neuromorphic charge transport and co-design |

What ties these strands together is not “we used a conductive polymer.” It is a laboratory habit: **process PEDOT:PSS as aqueous soft matter, then let ions and electrons both do work** — in a pore, a fiber lumen, a flowing slurry, a cell niche, or a synaptic conductance state.

PEDOT:PSS, in this body of work, is less a specialty coating than a materials platform that keeps reappearing wherever our group needs something simultaneously soft, wet, shapeable, and electrically alive.
